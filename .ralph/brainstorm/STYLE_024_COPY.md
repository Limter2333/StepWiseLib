# STYLE_024: Zen Minimal (禅意极简) — AI Multi-Agent System UI Copy

## Aesthetic Direction
Zen. Meditation. Minimalist Eastern aesthetics. The interface as breath, not tool. White space as invitation. Absence as presence. The space between things holds meaning.

---

## PHILOSOPHY

> "The cup does not hold the tea. The tea finds its way in. The empty vessel serves. Stillness is not absence. It is presence waiting. When you send, you do not push — you release. Let go. The breath does the work."
