# STYLE_026 — Glitch Core Copy Brainstorm
## 故障艺术 AI Multi-Agent System UI Copy

---

## 1. Concept & Vision

Glitch Core channels the aesthetic of digital corruption — the beauty found in system failures, data decay, and error states. UI text becomes a dialogue between broken systems and the users who inhabit them. Every label, button, and status message should feel like intercepted transmissions from a corrupted machine intelligence. The tone is cold, technical, and slightly unsettling — yet oddly elegant.

> **数字腐败** — Digital corruption as aesthetic choice. Not chaos for chaos's sake, but the deliberate embrace of the beautiful imperfection inherent in digital systems.

## 8. Microcopy Principles

1. **Machine voice** — Write as if the system itself is speaking. Cold, precise, declarative.
2. **Corruption framing** — Normal operations are "transmissions," "packets," "processes." Failures are "corruption" and "anomalies."
3. **Bracketed placeholders** — Use `[ID]`, `[N]`, `[TIME]` as live variables in copy.
4. **Bilingual by default** — Every string appears in English first, Chinese second, separated by `/`.
5. **No apologies** — The system does not say "sorry." It reports, states, and directs.
6. **Warning over reassurance** — Prefer informative warnings ("may corrupt") over promises ("will work").
7. **Terminal aesthetic** — Copy often leads with `>` or `>>` to evoke command-line terminals.
